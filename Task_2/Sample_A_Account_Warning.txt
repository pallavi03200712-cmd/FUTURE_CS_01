Sample A — Urgent Account Lock Warning

Subject: Urgent: Your Account Will Be Locked

Email body:

Dear User,

We noticed suspicious activity on your account. To avoid account suspension, please verify your details immediately.

Verify Now: `http://secure-account-verify[.]com`

Failure to verify within 24 hours will result in permanent account lock.

Regards,  
Security Team

Initial observations:
- Generic greeting: “Dear User”
- Urgent language and threat of account suspension
- Request to verify account details immediately
- Suspicious-looking link

Preliminary classification: Phishing indicators present; treat as potentially phishing.

Evidence limitation: The sender address and raw email headers were not provided, so they have not been verified.
