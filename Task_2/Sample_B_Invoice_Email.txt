Sample B — Unexpected Invoice Email

Subject: Invoice Payment Pending — Action Required

Email body:

Hello,

Please find the attached invoice for your recent purchase. Our records show that payment is still pending.

Please review the invoice and confirm the payment details with your usual supplier contact before taking action.

Thank you,
Accounts Team

Initial observations:
- An unexpected invoice may be suspicious if no purchase was made.
- The message mentions a payment but does not identify the supplier.
- No sender address or attachment details have been provided.

Preliminary classification: Suspicious — more information is needed to determine whether the email is legitimate or phishing.

Evidence limitation: This is a fictional training example. No real sender, attachment, or email headers have been verified.
